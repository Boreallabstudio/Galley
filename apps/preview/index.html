Ascent A skin for Video.js 10

You are the flight controller.

The seek bar is a flight path across the film. Volume and speed are engine dials. Three seconds after launch the console lifts away and the film is clear.

On the console

  1. Flight pathA real Video.js time slider. The chapters are events along it; drag the craft or press an event to fly there.
  2. Engine dialsVolume and speed. Turn, drag or scroll; the throat of the engine glows from ember to white-hot.
  3. Launch, Hold, ResumePlay is a launch with an ignition flare. Buffering is a hold, and the Downlink lamp shows the real connection speed.
  4. Mission clockElapsed time, with a countdown to the next event. At the end the craft settles into a parking orbit.

Keys and touch

  • Space or K launch and hold
  • ← → 5 seconds
  • ↑ ↓ volume
  • N next event
  • F full screen
  • ? flight manual
  • On a phone, double tap either side to skip 10 seconds
